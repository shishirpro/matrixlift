VOLKSLIFT is a Multi-National Joint-Venture Elevator Company of Germany-China-Switzerland.

VOLKSLIFT Group (1935) shifted to China in 2003 and entered into a Joint-Venture with Huzhou Xunda Elevator Co., Ltd. (1963).

Later in 2020, the world renowned elevator giant from Switzerland joined in the Joint Venture thus VOLKSLIFT become one of the most competitive Joint-Ventures in China’s elevator industry. VOLKSLIFT is committed to provide safe, reliable, convenient and efficient transportation solutions for all kinds of buildings.

The factory is located in Xitai Science & Technology Industrial Park at Huzhou City, Zhejiang, China. The manufacturing plant covers 100,000m2 area with a 108m Test Tower comprising 6 test shafts and a high speed elevator of 7m/s in it. About 1000 work force working hard to produce 30,000+ unit elevators annually in an European sourced fully automatic robotic production line under German Management & Supervision.
