MATRIX is our own brand Trade Mark Registered in Bangladesh.

The Prime Series MATRIX LIFT provides premium elevator solutions with enhanced aesthetics and performance.

ELEVATES is our Own Brand Trade Mark Registratered in Bangladesh.

ELEVATES conceptualized, designed and developed to make it budget friendly for the low segment market without compromising on safety and functionality.

The supply split in to two parts:

1) Imported Items - Major & Safety Components:

  • Control Panel
  • Traction Machine
  • Complete Cabin
  • Door Operator & Header
  • Speed Governor
  • Safety Gear
  • Hydraulic Buffer
  • Traction Rope
  • Traveling Cable
  • COP & LOPs
  • Car Top Inspection Box
  • Safety Switches
  • Guide Rails

2) Local Value Addition - Non-safety components:

  • Counter Weight Frame & Blocks
  • Traction Machine Base
  • All Brackets
  • Limit Bars
  • Speed Governor Rope
  • Sill Guard
  • Car Top Railing
  • Pit Ladder etc.

Standards, Safety and comfort are maintained for all the components.
