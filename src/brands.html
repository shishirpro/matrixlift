VOLKSLIFT

Precision in motion. Excellence in elevation.

VOLKSLIFT is a multi-national joint-venture elevator company of Germany–China–Switzerland. VOLKSLIFT Group (1935) shifted to China in 2003 and entered into a joint venture with Huzhou Xunda Elevator Co., Ltd. (1963).

In 2020 the world-renowned elevator giant from Switzerland joined the joint venture, making VOLKSLIFT one of the most competitive joint ventures in China’s elevator industry — committed to safe, reliable, convenient and efficient transportation for all kinds of buildings.
