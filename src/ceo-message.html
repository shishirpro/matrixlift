MATRIX is more than just a provider of vertical transportation solutions — we are focused on crafting experiences in the way people interact with their environment.

We take immense pride in our growing presence in Bangladesh’s infrastructure development, and we are committed to upholding high standards.

Affordability meets quality

MATRIX management, with decades of experience in the lift industry, is focused on redefining vertical mobility in our country by offering innovative, cost-effective and customised solutions — bridging the gap between affordability and quality, so that businesses, residential complexes and institutions have access to world-class lift systems tailored to their unique needs.

Long-term value

We have optimised our supply chain to provide cutting-edge technology at competitive prices. Our focus is not just selling lifts, but delivering long-term value through exceptional service, robust engineering and customer-centric solutions.

As we move forward, our goal is to continue innovating and expanding our offerings to better serve our clients and contribute to the nation’s progress.

We invite our valued patrons and customers to avail our services and feel the difference.

Sincerely yours,

Emdad Ur Rahman
