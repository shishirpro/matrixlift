About company

Family owned. Second generation. Four decades deep.

MATRIX is a family owned and managed company with the second generation, backed by about four decades of industry experience of our Founder Managing Director & CEO at home and abroad.

The company is dedicated to the vertical transportation solution industry — mainly all sorts of lifting equipment. We are very much concerned about the safety, reliability and comfort of all our products, as these are mostly human carriers.

Our market & our answer

Built to keep pace with Bangladesh.

THEN

The use of elevators in Bangladesh has grown significantly over the past decades, paralleling urbanisation and economic development — from manually operated single-speed lifts to modern smart lifts.

2000s

High-rise construction rose rapidly with population growth and economic expansion. Demand for lifts surged, bringing international brands from China, Korea and other countries.

NOW

With insight into the market’s growth and trend, we registered two brands in Bangladesh — and, with a world-class German-originated brand, offer a product mix for the entire market.
