Our market

Keeping pace with the trend.

THEN

The use of elevators in Bangladesh has grown significantly over the past few decades, paralleling the country’s urbanization and economic development. From Manually operated single speed lifts in early days to modern smart lifts, the industry has evolved to meet the increasing demand for vertical transportation.

2000s

The 2000s marked a rapid increase in high-rise construction due to population growth and economic expansion. The demand for lifts surged, leading to the entry of various international brands from China, Korea and other countries.

NOW

We have insight of the growth and trend of our market and have designed our Company to keep pace with the trend. With that lead, we have registered two different brand in Bangladesh to keep control over the requirement of our market alongside the expertise earned over a period of time.
