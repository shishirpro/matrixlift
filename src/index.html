AboutCompany

MATRIX is a family owned and managed Company with the Second Generation backed by about 4 decades industry experience of our Founder Managing Director & CEO in home and abroad.

The Company is dedicated in the Vertical Transportation Solution Industry mainly all sorts of lifting equipments. We are very much concern about the safety, reliability and comfort of all our products as these are mostly a Human Carrier.
